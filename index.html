    OktaAuth0KeycloakOIDCSAMLSCIM

  3. 2025 — now current

    Senior Software Engineer

    Multi-tenant hospital & enterprise ERP platform

    Platform standards and the hardest cross-cutting pieces of a monolith being carved into domain services.

    • Service-extraction standard: own database, tenant that fails closed, idempotent consumers, dead-letter over delay-topic retries.
    • Tenant isolation in depth: PostgreSQL row-level security, two-role model, 404-not-403 on cross-tenant loads.
    • Shift-left AppSec every service inherits — Semgrep custom rules, Trivy, gitleaks, Checkov, Cosign — shipped by GitOps with Argo CD.
    More
    • Transactional outbox → Kafka → idempotent projections; absolute values on the wire.
    • Field-level encryption, PHI access logging, hash-chained financial audit trail.
    • Regulatory engines: versioned statutory thresholds, national health reporting (DHIS2), tax e-invoicing, insurance claims.
    • Playwright end-to-end spec mandatory for every form; ArchUnit rules that fail the build.
    • AI-assisted engineering: agent guidance in the repo, multi-agent audits, token-discipline skills.
